A PLACE THAT STAYS WITH YOU.

Tucked along an untouched volcanic fault line in the warm South Pacific, Aurelia Isles is an archipelago of eleven inhabited coral islands and over forty secret lagoons. Unlike commercialized resort hubs, Aurelia preserves an ancient pact between ocean, rainforest, and islander heritage.

Wake to the scent of wild frangipani and wood-fired coconut bread, hike mist-draped basalt ridges to 12th-century celestial stone observatories, and dine on line-caught golden mahi beneath lantern-lit banyan canopies. Here, hospitality is not a service—it is an ancestral kinship called “Aloha Va’a” (the shared canoe).

ESSENTIAL TRAVEL GUIDE

☀️ Best Time to Visit

May through October

Dry trade-wind season brings 28°C highs, 45-meter underwater visibility, and peak oceanic manta migrations.

✈️ Getting Here

Seaplane via Port Solaris

Direct international flights land at Solaris International (ARI), followed by a 25-minute scenic twin-otter flight to your atoll.

🛂 Entry & Eco-Pledge

30-Day Visa on Arrival

All visitors sign the Aurelia Reef Pledge upon arrival; reef-safe mineral sunscreen is provided complimentary at all villas.

💎 Currency & Language

Aurelian Crown & USD

English, French, and indigenous Aurelian are spoken widely. Contactless cards and USD are accepted across all islands.
